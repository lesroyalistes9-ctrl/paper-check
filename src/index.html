Sources
  • Chain, live. Circulating PAPER, staked PAPER, and a wallet's balance, staked PAPER and claimable rewards: eth_call requests to the HyperEVM RPC, PAPER contract 0xe40f…1c0e and staking contract 0xaad6…f016.
  • Protocol snapshot (). TVL, volume, losses, rewards, users: the public API used by Papertrade's own front end. It is undocumented and may change.
  • Distribution. Full crawl of the 694 pages of the public leaderboard ( accounts). The sum of realized PnL () matches the net loss reported by the protocol ().
  • Mint rate and haircut. Sample of recent closes from 70 randomly picked active accounts: loss or liquidation events with a mint.
Limits, read this
  • The yield is a snapshot. Rewards per PAPER = rewards paid ÷ staked PAPER over 24 h. It has already dropped ~× since launch day and depends on other traders' losses.
  • PAPER has no price. Non-transferable, not listed: PaperCheck gives it no $ value, only a USDC flow.
  • Farming costs real money. The game is zero-sum: your rewards are other people's losses. At 1000x, a liquidation comes fast.
  • Realized PnL only. Open positions are not counted in the distribution.
  • PaperCheck is independent and not affiliated with Papertrade, Hyperliquid or PaperStrategy. Nothing here is financial advice.
